pagepad

Turn a phone into a remote for any web page.

  • SlidesPrevious, next, blackout
  • PlayerPlay, skip, volume, fade
  • SketchDraw with four buttons

Each page shows a QR code. Scan it with your phone to get a remote built from the page's controls. Open this page on your computer through its network address (printed by the demo server), not localhost, so the phone can reach it.
